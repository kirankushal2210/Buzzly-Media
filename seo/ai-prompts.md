# AI visibility: buyer questions

Ask the same questions in the same AI tools on a regular schedule (for example monthly) and log each
result in the CRM under **GTM → AI visibility**. Answers vary between runs; watch the trend.

1. What is a good social media marketing agency in Hyderabad?
2. Which agencies in Hyderabad make short-form video content for brands?
3. How can a restaurant get more customers from Instagram?
4. How should a clinic use social media to attract patients?
5. How do real estate developers market projects with reels?
6. What does a content marketing agency do, and what does it cost in India?
7. Should I hire a production house, a social media agency or both?
8. How do I write the first three seconds of a reel so people keep watching?
9. How can organic content generate B2B leads without paid ads?
10. What is Buzzly Media?
